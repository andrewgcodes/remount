# Code of conduct

Everyone participating in this project, in issues, pull requests, reviews,
discussions and any other project space, is expected to:

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not people. No harassment, personal
  attacks, discriminatory language or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Be precise. Report what you observed, with the commit, host and command.
  Say "I don't know" rather than guessing, and mark an unverified claim as
  unverified.
- Be constructive. Review to make the change better, not to win. Explain
  the why behind a request.
- Respect other people's time and work. Read the existing docs and ADRs
  before proposing a change to a boundary they already decide.

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions and comments that do
not follow this, and may temporarily or permanently exclude anyone who
repeatedly breaks it.

To report a conduct problem, contact the maintainers privately through the
repository's GitHub contact options rather than in a public thread. Reports
are handled confidentially.

This document is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant; we keep it
short so it is read.
